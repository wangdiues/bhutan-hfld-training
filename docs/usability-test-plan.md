# Usability test plan (task M7-07)

**Goal:** check that forestry staff in Bhutan can find their way, follow a lesson, and use the labs without help.
**Participants:** at least 2 (ideally 3–5) Bhutanese forestry or GIS staff, for example from the Department of Forests and Park Services. Mix of Earth Engine beginners and users.
**Format:** 45–60 minutes each, in person or by video call, on the participant's own device (include at least one phone). Think aloud; the facilitator does not help unless the participant is stuck for more than 2 minutes.

## Before

- Consent: what is recorded, and that the site is being tested, not the person.
- Note: device, browser, connection speed, Earth Engine experience (none, some, regular).

## Tasks

| # | Task | Success when | Watch for |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | From the home page, find out what the course covers and how long it takes. | Finds Start here or the workflow map, and states the five days. | Does the workflow map make sense? |
| 2 | Run the first script (Start here → Hello Bhutan) in the Code Editor. | Script runs and shows the Thimphu composite. | Earth Engine registration problems; copying code. |
| 3 | In "Spectral indices", find what NDVI is and try the index explorer. | Explains NDVI in own words; moves a slider. | Theory panels, sliders on a phone. |
| 4 | Open the Day 3 slides and find the slide about how a decision tree works. | Finds it with arrows or swipe. | Slide player controls. |
| 5 | In the accuracy and area estimator, change one count and read the adjusted forest area and its 90% CI. | Reads both numbers correctly. | Table size on a phone; the CI wording. |
| 6 | Find which TREES section says how uncertainty is calculated. | Finds Section 8 via the ART-TREES lesson or search. | Search, navigation. |
| 7 | Mark a lesson complete and find your progress. | Sees the sidebar progress panel. | Discoverability. |
| 8 | Without a connection (airplane mode, after visiting), open a lesson you visited. | Page loads. | Offline behaviour. |

## After (5 minutes)

- What was hardest? What would you change first?
- Would you use this to prepare a real TREES monitoring report? Why or why not?
- Rate overall ease of use, 1–5.

## Logging

Each problem becomes a GitHub issue labelled `usability`, with: task number, what happened, severity (blocker, major, minor), device. Fixes are tracked under M7-08.
