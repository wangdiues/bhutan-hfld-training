## How it works

<Theory title="Area-adjusted estimates" more="/explanation/area-estimation/">
With stratum weights $W_i = A_i / A$ and $n_{ij}$ samples mapped as $i$ with reference class $j$, the estimated area proportion of each cell is $\hat p_{ij} = W_i \, n_{ij} / n_{i\cdot}$. The adjusted area of class $j$ is $\hat A_j = A \sum_i \hat p_{ij}$, with standard error
$$ SE(\hat p_{\cdot j}) = \sqrt{\sum_i W_i^2 \, \frac{\hat p_{j|i}(1 - \hat p_{j|i})}{n_{i\cdot} - 1}}, \qquad \hat p_{j|i} = n_{ij} / n_{i\cdot} $$
<Cite id="olofsson2014" at="Eqs. 9–10" />. The standard error of the area is $SE(\hat A_j) = A \, SE(\hat p_{\cdot j})$ (Eq. 11), and the confidence interval is $\hat A_j \pm z \, SE(\hat A_j)$, with $z = 1.6449$ for 90%.
</Theory>

- **User's accuracy** is the share of samples mapped as a class that really are that class. Low user's accuracy means commission errors.
- **Producer's accuracy** is the share of a reference class that the map got right. Low producer's accuracy means omission errors. Under stratified sampling, the **area-weighted** version is the unbiased one <Cite id="olofsson2014" />. The workbook's version, from raw sample counts, is shown too, for comparison.
- **Uncertainty** is the half-width of the confidence interval as a percentage of the adjusted area. If the programme you report to sets a maximum uncertainty, type it into the optional threshold box to get a pass or fail for each class.

<Callout type="bhutan" title="What the workbook example shows">
In the workbook, the Non-Forest stratum has no samples. Its user's accuracy cannot be computed, and the adjusted areas cover only the sampled half of the map: they add up to about 2.69 million ha instead of 5.37 million ha. The lab flags this and will not report a total. Every stratum needs samples.
</Callout>

<Callout type="mistake" title="Common mistakes">
- **Swapping rows and columns.** Rows are the map class (the stratum the sample came from); columns are what the interpreter saw.
- **Reporting the mapped area.** Pixel counting carries the map's errors. Report the adjusted area and its confidence interval.
- **Using the workbook variance rule for reporting.** It leaves out cells with a single sample. Olofsson et al. keep them, so the lab uses their rule unless you choose otherwise.
- **Pasting CEO data with other column names.** Rename the map-class and interpreted-class columns to `map` and `reference` first.
</Callout>

The lab's maths is in `src/lib/stats/accuracyArea.ts` and `src/lib/stats/accuracyLab.ts`. Automated tests check that the workbook example reproduces every output of the workbook to within one part in a million, and that bad input gives a message, never NaN or Infinity.
