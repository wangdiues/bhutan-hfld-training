## What Day 2 covered

- [Fundamentals of remote sensing](/bhutan-hfld-training/day-2/remote-sensing-fundamentals/): reflectance, spectral signatures and the four resolutions.
- [Spectral indices and functions](/bhutan-hfld-training/day-2/spectral-indices/): NDVI, NDWI, NDMI, EVI, SAVI; index functions mapped over collections.
- [Mosaics and composites](/bhutan-hfld-training/day-2/mosaics-and-composites/): mosaic vs composite; atmospheric, BRDF and terrain correction.
- [Sentinel-2 MRV composite](/bhutan-hfld-training/day-2/sentinel-2-mrv-composite/): Jan–Apr and Oct–Dec medians with Cloud Score+, TDOM and terrain correction.
- [Landsat MRV composite](/bhutan-hfld-training/day-2/landsat-mrv-composite/): the same product from Landsat 8/9.
- [Importing and exporting data](/bhutan-hfld-training/day-2/import-and-export/): assets, Drive exports and videos.
