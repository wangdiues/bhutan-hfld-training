## What you will learn

<CardGrid>
	<Card title="Day 1 · Earth Engine foundations" icon="laptop">
		The Code Editor, JavaScript basics, image collections, filtering and reducers.
	</Card>
	<Card title="Day 2 · Composites" icon="seti:image">
		Cloud and shadow masking, terrain correction, spectral indices and seasonal composites for Bhutan.
	</Card>

	</Card>
	<Card title="Day 4 · Land cover & embeddings" icon="puzzle">

	</Card>
	<Card title="Day 5 · Accuracy & area" icon="approve-check">
		Stratified sampling, confusion matrices and area-adjusted estimates with 90% confidence intervals.
	</Card>
