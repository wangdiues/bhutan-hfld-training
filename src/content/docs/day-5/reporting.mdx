The end product of the course is not a map but a set of **numbers with their evidence**: the area of each activity (for example forest loss) for each period, with confidence intervals, and a record that lets a reviewer check how they were made. When maps feed national reporting, they are often checked by an independent reviewer, so transparency and reproducibility matter as much as the numbers themselves.


## The area table

Report, for each period and each activity, the **area-adjusted estimate** from the reference sample, not the mapped area. A clear table has one row per class or activity:

| Class / activity | Mapped area (ha) | Adjusted area (ha) | SE (ha) | 90% CI (ha) | Uncertainty (± %) | User's accuracy | Producer's accuracy (area-weighted) |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Stable forest | … | … | … | … to … | … | … | … |
| Forest loss (deforestation) | … | … | … | … to … | … | … | … |
| Stable non-forest | … | … | … | … to … | … | … | … |
| **Total** | = map total | = map total | | | | | |

The [accuracy and area calculator](/lulc-mapping-training/labs/accuracy-area-estimator/) produces all of these columns and downloads them as a CSV. Alongside the table, report:

- the **confusion matrix** in sample counts, and the **stratum weights** (mapped areas);
- the **sample size per stratum** and how it was allocated;
- the **period** (start and end dates) and the **forest definition** used;
- the **estimator** (stratified, Olofsson et al. 2014) <Cite id="olofsson2014" /> and the confidence level (the course uses 90%, z = 1.6449; check the level your standard requires).

<Callout type="mistake" title="Totals must add up">
The adjusted areas of all classes should add up to the total mapped area. If they don't, a stratum probably has no samples (see the [calculator](/lulc-mapping-training/labs/accuracy-area-estimator/)): fix the sample, don't adjust the numbers.
</Callout>

## The assumptions log

Every choice that affects the numbers belongs in a log, written as you go, not at the end. A simple table works. The rows below are an illustrative example (the dates are invented; a minimum of 150 per stratum is the site's alternative to the training's 80):

| Date | Step | Decision | Why | Effect / evidence |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 2026-05-10 | Composite | Jan–Apr and Oct–Dec windows | monsoon months have too few clear images | monthly image counts |
| 2026-05-12 | Covariates | bands scaled to 0–1 reflectance before EVI/SAVI | constants assume 0–1 | — |
| 2026-05-14 | Assemblage | forest first, threshold 0.50 | matches the forest definition | primitive accuracy plot |
| 2026-05-16 | Sampling | minimum 150 per stratum | deforestation stratum needs enough samples | calculator output |
| 2026-05-20 | Interpretation | mixed plots labelled by majority class | the project's response design ([Collect Earth Online](/lulc-mapping-training/day-4/collect-earth-online/)) | double-interpretation agreement |

Include, at least: the forest definition and how it maps to classes and thresholds; image windows and cloud thresholds; masks (cloud, shadow, snow); covariates; training data sources and dates; classifier settings and seeds; assemblage order and thresholds; strata, sample sizes and the seed of the sample; the response design; and any corrections made afterwards.

## The reproducibility checklist

A reviewer, or a colleague next year, should be able to rerun the work and get the same numbers.

- ☐ **Code** for every step is saved in a shared Earth Engine repository (or Git), with the final version tagged or dated.
- ☐ **Assets** (boundaries, composites, primitives, map, samples) are kept, shared read-only, and named with source, class and year (for example `ls_Forest_2025`).
- ☐ **Versions** are recorded: Earth Engine dataset IDs (for example `COPERNICUS/S2_SR_HARMONIZED`, `GOOGLE/SATELLITE_EMBEDDING/V1/ANNUAL`), and the version of any guidance or standard followed.
- ☐ **Random seeds** are fixed and recorded (classifiers, training/validation splits, the stratified sample).
- ☐ **The reference sample** (plots and samples), the Collect Earth Online export and the interpreter notes are archived unchanged.
- ☐ **The calculator inputs and outputs** (strata areas, confusion matrix, results CSV) are archived.
- ☐ **The assumptions log** is complete and dated.
- ☐ **A short method description** connects all of the above, in the order of the [workflow](/lulc-mapping-training/start-here/).

<Callout type="why">
Why this matters: a number that can't be reproduced can't be verified. Seeds, versions and archived samples turn "we got 13,304 ha" into a result anyone can check.
</Callout>

<Callout type="bhutan">
Bhutan context: the work spans several agencies and years. Keeping one shared Earth Engine project for the land-cover work, with a folder per year and a single assumptions log, makes each new monitoring period faster and keeps the time series consistent.
</Callout>

## Common mistakes

- **Reporting mapped areas** instead of adjusted areas with confidence intervals.
- **Unrecorded seeds**: rerunning gives a slightly different map or sample.
- **Assets in personal folders** that disappear when someone leaves.
- **Changing methods between periods** without recording it, so change in the numbers reflects method, not land.
