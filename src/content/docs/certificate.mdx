When you have passed the quizzes for all five days, you can download a one-page certificate (PDF) with your name and the date. It is made in your browser: your name and scores are not sent anywhere.

<Certificate />

The certificate records self-paced study on this website. It is not an accreditation or a formal qualification, and it is not issued by Conservation International or by the ART-TREES programme. Quiz scores are kept in this browser only, so use the same browser for the whole course.
