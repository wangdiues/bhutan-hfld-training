## What Day 3 covered

- [Random Forest theory](/bhutan-hfld-training/day-3/random-forest/): decision trees, entropy, information gain, bagging and random feature selection.
- [Calculating covariates](/bhutan-hfld-training/day-3/covariates/): seasonal indices, Tasseled Cap, terrain and water layers.
- [Forest primitives](/bhutan-hfld-training/day-3/forest-primitives/): one Random Forest per class in probability mode.
- [Change detection and loss strata](/bhutan-hfld-training/day-3/change-strata/): stable forest, loss and non-forest strata from two dates.
