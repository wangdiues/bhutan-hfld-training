## Bands

The course's composites keep six bands from each sensor. Band numbers differ between the two, so check this table before writing an index.

| Name | Sentinel-2 band | Centre (nm) | Pixel | Landsat 8/9 band | Range (nm) | Pixel |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Blue | B2 | 490 | 10 m | B2 | 452–512 | 30 m |
| Green | B3 | 560 | 10 m | B3 | 533–590 | 30 m |
| Red | B4 | 665 | 10 m | B4 | 636–673 | 30 m |
| Red edge 1–3 | B5, B6, B7 | 705, 740, 783 | 20 m | — | — | — |
| Near infrared (NIR) | B8 (B8A narrow, 865) | 842 | 10 m (B8A 20 m) | B5 | 851–879 | 30 m |
| Shortwave infrared 1 (SWIR1) | B11 | 1610 | 20 m | B6 | 1566–1651 | 30 m |
| Shortwave infrared 2 (SWIR2) | B12 | 2190 | 20 m | B7 | 2107–2294 | 30 m |

The course composites use Sentinel-2 bands B2, B3, B4, B8, B11 and B12, and Landsat bands B2–B7, each with a `_jan_apr` and an `_oct_dec` suffix: 12 bands in all.

### Scaling to reflectance (0–1)

| Dataset | Stored as | To get reflectance |
|---|---|---|
| [Sentinel-2 SR](https://developers.google.com/earth-engine/datasets/catalog/COPERNICUS_S2_SR_HARMONIZED) (`COPERNICUS/S2_SR_HARMONIZED`) | integers, reflectance × 10,000 | `image.divide(10000)` |
| [Landsat 8/9 Collection 2 Level-2](https://developers.google.com/earth-engine/datasets/catalog/LANDSAT_LC08_C02_T1_L2) (`SR_B*` bands) | scaled integers | `image.multiply(0.0000275).add(-0.2)` |
| Course composites (`…_int16`) | integers, reflectance × 10,000 | `image.divide(10000)` |

Normalized-difference indices (NDVI, NDWI, …) give the same answer whether or not you divide by 10,000, because a pure multiplication cancels out. An offset does not cancel, so apply Landsat Level-2's `add(-0.2)` before any index. EVI, SAVI, IBI and Tasseled Cap need 0–1 reflectance.

## Spectral indices

Formulas use the names from the band table. [Spectral indices](/bhutan-hfld-training/day-2/spectral-indices/) and [Calculating covariates](/bhutan-hfld-training/day-3/covariates/) show the code.

| Index | Formula | Sentinel-2 | Landsat 8/9 | High values mean |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| NDVI <Cite id="tucker1979" /> | (NIR − Red) / (NIR + Red) | (B8 − B4) / (B8 + B4) | (B5 − B4) / (B5 + B4) | dense green vegetation |
| EVI | 2.5 × (NIR − Red) / (NIR + 6 Red − 7.5 Blue + 1) | B8, B4, B2 | B5, B4, B2 | green vegetation, less saturated than NDVI over dense forest |
| SAVI (L = 0.5) | 1.5 × (NIR − Red) / (NIR + Red + 0.5) | B8, B4 | B5, B4 | vegetation, with less soil effect where cover is sparse |
| NDMI | (NIR − SWIR1) / (NIR + SWIR1) | (B8 − B11) / (B8 + B11) | (B5 − B6) / (B5 + B6) | moist canopy |
| NBR | (NIR − SWIR2) / (NIR + SWIR2) | (B8 − B12) / (B8 + B12) | (B5 − B7) / (B5 + B7) | healthy vegetation; a drop between dates marks fire or clearing |
| NDWI | (Green − NIR) / (Green + NIR) | (B3 − B8) / (B3 + B8) | (B3 − B5) / (B3 + B5) | open water |
| MNDWI | (Green − SWIR1) / (Green + SWIR1) | (B3 − B11) / (B3 + B11) | (B3 − B6) / (B3 + B6) | open water, separated better from built land |
| NDBI | (SWIR1 − NIR) / (SWIR1 + NIR) | (B11 − B8) / (B11 + B8) | (B6 − B5) / (B6 + B5) | built-up and bare land |
| IBI | (NDBI − (SAVI + MNDWI) / 2) / (NDBI + (SAVI + MNDWI) / 2) | from the three indices | from the three indices | built-up land, but in this form the denominator can cross zero, so values are unbounded; use it as a covariate, not on its own |

In Earth Engine, write a normalized difference as `image.normalizedDifference(['B8', 'B4'])`, which computes (first − second) / (first + second).

:::caution[Common mistakes]
- Using Sentinel-2 band numbers on Landsat: Landsat's NIR is B5, not B8.
- Computing EVI or SAVI on reflectance × 10,000. The constants in the formulas assume 0–1.
- Expecting a fixed threshold, such as "NDVI > 0.4 is forest", to work everywhere. Check it against samples for each season and region of Bhutan.
:::

## Further reading

### For the whole course

- *Cloud-Based Remote Sensing with Google Earth Engine: Fundamentals and Applications* (Cardille, Crowley, Saah and Clinton, eds., Springer, 2024). Open access, with code for every chapter: [eefabook.org](https://www.eefabook.org/).
- [Earth Engine community tutorials](https://developers.google.com/earth-engine/tutorials/community/explore) (Google).
- [OpenMRV](https://openmrv.org/): open-source tools and tutorials for forest monitoring and MRV.
- [FAO e-learning Academy](https://elearning.fao.org/): free courses on REDD+, forest monitoring and estimating uncertainty in forest emissions.
