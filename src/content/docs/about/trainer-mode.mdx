The course was first taught as a five-day workshop in May 2026. If you are running it as a workshop, turn on trainer mode: each lesson then shows, at the end, when it ran in that timetable, who led it, and notes for facilitating that day.

Trainer mode only changes what this browser shows. It is not a login, and there are no answer keys behind it: the course is public, and practice solutions are already on the lesson pages.

Timetable adapted from the agenda of the Bhutan ART-TREES HFLD Readiness Training (May 2026) by Ate Poortinga and Samuel, Conservation International. Breaks and lunch are left out. Some lessons were added after the training (for example Change detection, Reporting and the Day 5 recap) and have no slot.
