## About this course

This website is a free, self-paced version of the **Bhutan ART-TREES HFLD Readiness Training**, a five-day workshop held in May 2026. It teaches how to produce the activity data for an ART-TREES high forest cover, low deforestation (HFLD) submission with Google Earth Engine: seasonal composites, land-cover primitives and maps, a stratified reference sample, and area estimates with 90% confidence intervals.

The course is not an official product of ART, Winrock International, Conservation International or the Royal Government of Bhutan. Its certificate records self-paced study; it is not an accreditation.

## Acknowledgements

**The training.** The course is built on the slides, exercises, scripts and calculators of the May 2026 training by **Ate Poortinga** and **Samuel** of **Conservation International**, adapted with their permission (granted October 2026). Each adapted lesson ends with a credit naming its source file and listing what was changed.

**Changes to the source.** Every correction to the original material (code fixes, renamed assets, Bhutan examples, corrected numbers and claims) is listed in the [changelog](https://github.com/wangdiues/bhutan-hfld-training/blob/main/CHANGELOG.md) under "Corrections to source material". Lessons marked *new content* were written for the website and are not part of the training.

**Standards and methods.** Requirements are cited from the ART TREES standard (Version 3.0, June 2026, with notes on Version 2.0), published by Architecture for REDD+ Transactions. The area-estimation methods follow Olofsson et al. (2014) and the RLCMS approach of Saah et al. (2020). Each page lists its references.

**Data.** The lessons use public datasets from the Google Earth Engine catalog: Sentinel-2 (Copernicus, ESA), Landsat (NASA/USGS), Cloud Score+, SRTM, JRC Global Surface Water, Hansen Global Forest Change, the Satellite Embedding dataset (Google DeepMind's AlphaEarth Foundations), Dynamic World, Open Buildings and FAO GAUL boundaries. Each is linked to its catalog page where it is used; see the [asset catalogue](/bhutan-hfld-training/reference/assets/). Project assets made during the training belong to their owners.

**Software.** Built with [Astro](https://astro.build/) and [Starlight](https://starlight.astro.build/); maths with [KaTeX](https://katex.org/); tested with [Vitest](https://vitest.dev/), axe and pa11y. The course was developed with the help of Claude Code, an AI coding assistant; content was checked against the source material and the standards before publication.

## How to cite

> Wangdi (2026). *Bhutan HFLD Training: an open course on ART-TREES HFLD activity data with Google Earth Engine.* https://wangdiues.github.io/bhutan-hfld-training/

The repository has a [CITATION.cff](https://github.com/wangdiues/bhutan-hfld-training/blob/main/CITATION.cff) file, so GitHub's "Cite this repository" button gives the same citation in other formats.

See [CONTRIBUTING](https://github.com/wangdiues/bhutan-hfld-training/blob/main/CONTRIBUTING.md) for how changes are reviewed.
