## Acknowledgements

**LULC Mapping Training** is built on the materials of a five-day training held in Bhutan in May 2026 (*Bhutan ART-TREES HFLD Readiness Training*), designed and taught by:

- **Ate Poortinga**, Conservation International
- **Samuel**, Conservation International

Their slides, exercises, Earth Engine scripts and calculators are the foundation of this course. They are adapted here **with their permission** (granted October 2026). We are grateful to them, and to the participants of the May 2026 training, whose exercises and assets appear throughout.

This website is an independent adaptation. It is not an official product of Conservation International or of any other organisation named here, and its certificate is not an accreditation.

## Methods, data and software

**Methods.** Area estimation follows Olofsson et al. (2014); the land-cover method follows the RLCMS approach of Saah et al. (2020). Each page lists its references.

**Data.** The lessons use public datasets from the Google Earth Engine catalog: Sentinel-2 (Copernicus, ESA), Landsat (NASA/USGS), Cloud Score+, SRTM, JRC Global Surface Water, Hansen Global Forest Change, the Satellite Embedding dataset (Google DeepMind's AlphaEarth Foundations), Dynamic World, Open Buildings and FAO GAUL boundaries. Each is linked to its catalog page where it is used; see the [asset catalogue](/lulc-mapping-training/reference/assets/). Project assets made during the training belong to their owners.

**Software.** Built with [Astro](https://astro.build/) and [Starlight](https://starlight.astro.build/); maths with [KaTeX](https://katex.org/); tested with [Vitest](https://vitest.dev/), axe and pa11y. The course was developed with the help of Claude Code, an AI coding assistant; content was checked against the source material before publication.
