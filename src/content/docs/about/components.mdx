## Theory panels

<Theory title="NDVI" more="/explanation/" open>
NDVI contrasts near-infrared, which healthy leaves reflect strongly, with red, which chlorophyll absorbs <Cite id="tucker1979" />:

$$
\text{NDVI} = \frac{\rho_{\text{NIR}} - \rho_{\text{Red}}}{\rho_{\text{NIR}} + \rho_{\text{Red}}}
$$
</Theory>

<Theory title="Area-adjusted estimate (Olofsson et al. 2014)">
With map-class weights $W_i = A_{m,i} / A_{tot}$ and sample counts $n_{ij}$, the estimated area proportion of reference class $j$ and its standard error are <Cite id="olofsson2014" at="eqs. 9–10" />:

$$
\hat{p}_{\cdot j} = \sum_i W_i \frac{n_{ij}}{n_{i\cdot}}, \qquad
S(\hat{p}_{\cdot j}) = \sqrt{\sum_i W_i^2 \, \frac{\frac{n_{ij}}{n_{i\cdot}}\left(1 - \frac{n_{ij}}{n_{i\cdot}}\right)}{n_{i\cdot} - 1}}
$$

The 90% confidence interval of the area is $\hat{A}_j \pm 1.645 \, A_{tot} \, S(\hat{p}_{\cdot j})$.
</Theory>
