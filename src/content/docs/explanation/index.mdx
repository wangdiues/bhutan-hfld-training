The lessons teach by doing and keep theory short. These pages explain the ideas behind each step. Lessons link here from their **Theory** panels.

- [How satellites see land cover](/bhutan-hfld-training/explanation/remote-sensing-physics/): reflectance, spectral signatures and why indices work.
- [Compositing and terrain correction](/bhutan-hfld-training/explanation/compositing-and-terrain-correction/): cloud and shadow masks, median composites, SCS+C and BRDF.
- [How Random Forest works](/bhutan-hfld-training/explanation/random-forest/): decision trees, bagging and probabilities.
- [RLCMS primitives and assemblage](/bhutan-hfld-training/explanation/rlcms-primitives-and-assemblage/): one probability layer per class, combined by rules.
- [Satellite embeddings](/bhutan-hfld-training/explanation/embeddings/): what the 64 numbers mean and how similarity works.
- [Design-based area estimation](/bhutan-hfld-training/explanation/area-estimation/): why areas come from a probability sample, and the Olofsson estimators.
- [TREES 2.0 → 3.0 for HFLD](/bhutan-hfld-training/explanation/trees-2-to-3/) (being written): what changed in the standard.
